# Notes skill

Record durable project memory in the project vault at `<repo>/obsidian/`.
This skill runs whenever agent work produces something worth remembering.

## What to record
- Decisions and their reasons (one line of why beats a paragraph of what).
- Todos and plans that outlive the session, with owners.
- Gotchas that cost real effort: commands, flags, paths, versions.

## Where
- Daily log: `obsidian/notes/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, appended under a timestamped heading with the session id.
- Keep entries short and factual. Never paste secrets.

## Search before writing
- Check the vault first (`/search` on the server, Analogy side pane on desktop) so notes stay deduplicated.

Analogous to OpenChamber project notes: the vault is the file-backed mirror.
